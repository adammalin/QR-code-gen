Operation and boundaries
The companion is bound to 127.0.0.1, checks Host/Origin, and accepts bounded JSON.
It does not fetch QR destinations or arbitrary external assets. Exports use
unique directories and exclusive writes; resources are restricted to known
generated files and verified against their original digest.

Default export folder: ~/Downloads/QR-Studio.
QR_STUDIO_PORT and QR_STUDIO_OUTPUT_DIR override the defaults for the first
companion instance. Later clients reuse that instance's settings.
The current editor is held only in memory. It is not a saved project; stopping
the companion loses its editor content. Files persist after it stops. Up to
200 artifact handles are retained per companion session.

Connected agents can receive editor content while access is enabled. Wi-Fi QR
exports contain the password. Neither browser storage nor application logs
persist QR content. QR exports are private local files; technical decode checks
do not replace scanning final printed/placed artwork or any required approval.
